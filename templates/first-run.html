First-time setup

Complete these four steps to unlock the manager. You can go back at any time. Optional features may stay off.

Telegram account and artist catalog

Connect your account, then paste the Table of Contents link for the group you want to use. Only creator links from that source are imported.

Where do I find the Table of Contents?

It is your Telegram group’s artist index: a message containing links to the individual artists’ topics.

  1. Open your group in Telegram. Check its pinned messages or look for a topic called “Table of Contents”, “TOC” or “Index”.
  2. Find the message listing the artists. Right-click it on a computer, or press and hold it on a phone, and choose the option to copy its message link.
  3. Paste the link below and click Import artists. Use the link to the index message, not a group invite link or an individual artist’s topic.

Can’t find the index or the copy-link option? Ask the group administrator for the Table of Contents message link. Your connected Telegram account must already have access to the group.
